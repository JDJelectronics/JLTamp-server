# Live demo

Try JLTamp in your browser at **https://demo.jltamp.nl**. Sign in with:

- user: `demo`
- password: `demo`

The demo runs the same public image you get from GHCR
(`ghcr.io/jltelectronics/jltamp-server`), with its own database and a small
library of freely licensed music. Everyone shares the demo account, and the
demo resets every night at 04:00 (Europe/Amsterdam). Deleting the account and
uploading images are switched off.

You can also connect a Subsonic client (Symfonium, Feishin, Tempo…) to
`https://demo.jltamp.nl` with the same user and password.
